About BrewOak (Artisan Sips, Crafted Ratios)

BrewOak is a dedicated vertical web utility and sensory calculator platform engineered for home baristas, roasters, and culinary fermentation enthusiasts worldwide.

1. Founding Philosophy: Artisan Precision

A truly great cup of coffee begins not with arbitrary intuition, but with scientifically reproducible ratios. BrewOak transforms complex thermodynamic parameters—volatile CO2 degassing schedules, hydraulic pour-over stages, and immersion brew mechanics—into intuitive, lightning-fast digital instruments. We believe home coffee craft should feel effortless, empowering anyone to reproduce specialty cafe standards with confidence.

2. Transparency & Zero-Bloat Principles

All extraction algorithms in BrewOak are formulated directly from the Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) Golden Cup Standard and World Brewers Cup championship recipes. Our tools run strictly client-side with zero unnecessary user tracking, prioritizing extreme performance, low data footprint, and total user privacy.
