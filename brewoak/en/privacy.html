Privacy Policy

BrewOak, operated by toomus ("we", "our", or "the Company"), respects your personal privacy and complies fully with international digital privacy frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Client-Side Computational Architecture

All calculations performed on BrewOak (brew ratios, water quantities, timer sequences, and degassing estimations) execute exclusively inside your web browser client. We do not store, log, transmit, or monetize your coffee parameters on external servers.
